Questions

Is my app's code uploaded?

No. Your browser opens the .ipa and keeps only Info.plist, privacy manifests and the headers of each binary (linked libraries, symbol names, SDK version, signing entitlements). Compiled code, images and assets stay on your machine. The trimmed file is scanned and not stored.

Does a clean scan guarantee approval?

No, and nobody can promise that. Only Apple approves apps. The scan catches problems that can be detected in a build. Many rejections come from metadata, design or business rules instead, which is what the paid review covers.

What do I get from a review, exactly?

A written report by email within 24 hours: every issue found, why it matters, and how to fix it, ordered by risk. For a rejection rescue you also get a ready-to-send reply to App Review.
