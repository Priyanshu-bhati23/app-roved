    Questions

    Is my app's code uploaded?

    No. Your browser opens the .ipa and keeps only Info.plist, privacy manifests and the headers of each binary (linked libraries, symbol names, SDK version, signing entitlements). Compiled code, images and assets stay on your machine. The trimmed file is scanned and not stored.

    Does a clean scan guarantee approval?

    No, and nobody can promise that. Only Apple approves apps. The scan catches problems that can be detected in a build. Many rejections come from App Store Connect setup, design or business rules instead, which is what the full review covers.

    Is the review done by a person?

    No, it's automated, which is why it's instant. Clear rules check your build and your answers against Apple's published App Review Guidelines, and AI writes the explanations and your reply to App Review using only those guidelines. If the AI is busy, you get the built-in explanation instead.

    What do I get from a review, exactly?

    A report on screen, right away: every issue found across your build, your App Store Connect setup and your description, why it matters, the guideline behind it, and how to fix it, ordered by risk. A rejection rescue adds what Apple's message means, the steps to fix it and a reply you can paste. Save either as a PDF.

    What if the review doesn't help?

    Tell us what it missed at the email below and we'll improve the checks. While reviews are free during the beta there's nothing to refund; once they're paid, you get your money back if it didn't help.
