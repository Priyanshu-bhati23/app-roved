## Account deletion in the app
Apps that let people create an account must let them start deleting it from inside the app, usually in account settings, and it should be easy to find. Deactivating or disabling the account is not enough: deletion has to remove the account record and its personal data, including content the person shared with others such as posts, photos and reviews, except what the law requires you to keep, and people should be told what is kept. If a website finishes the process, the app must link straight to that page. Apps outside highly regulated industries must not require a phone call, an email or a support chat to delete an account. Confirmation steps, such as entering a code sent to the email already on the account, are allowed, but making deletion unnecessarily hard is not. Deletion does not have to be instant: say how long it takes and confirm when it is done. Accounts created automatically, such as guest accounts, must be deletable too, and everyone must be able to delete their account wherever they live.

## Sign in with Apple and subscriptions when an account is deleted
When an account that used Sign in with Apple is deleted, revoke the user's tokens with the Sign in with Apple REST API, and handle Apple's server-to-server notifications for account changes. If the person has an auto-renewing subscription, tell them billing continues through Apple and ask them to cancel before deleting, using the system subscription management sheet or a link to it. You may schedule deletion for when the subscription ends, as long as there is also an option to delete right away.
